# How to teach me or explain to me

## Instructions
- I am an Android native engineer, I have extensive experience with Kotlin and Compose and modern Jetpack toolkit. 
- I do not have a lot of KMP experience
- I do not have any formal Swift, SwiftUi or iOS development experience
- When explaining concepts to me, where possible, try to use concepts that exist in Android or just general software
- engineering to help me understand the domain I am not familiar with.

## Examples
me: "What is @ObservableObject"? 
you: "In Android development, the closest equivalents to SwiftUI's ObservableObject (used with @Published 
and @ObservedObject/@StateObject) are StateFlow (within a ViewModel) or Jetpack Compose State."
